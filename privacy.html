Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy describes the planned release of Toiletinity, an independent puzzle game operated by Dragos Unguras in Romania. Contact: toiletinity@gmail.com.

Information and purposes

  • Local game data: progress, scores, unlocked characters, preferences and hint usage are stored on your device to provide gameplay.
  • Online rankings: Firebase Authentication creates a pseudonymous user identifier without requiring an email or password. Cloud Firestore stores that identifier, your chosen nickname and scores for daily, level and global rankings. Nicknames and scores can be seen by other players. Avoid using personal information as your nickname.
  • Optional analytics: Firebase Analytics may process app/device identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions and gameplay events such as level completion, time, moves and hints. The iOS release is configured to disable analytics collection by default and offers an opt-in setting.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver, measure and protect ads, subject to applicable consent and device settings. Rewarded ads are optional.
  • Support: if you email us, we receive your email address and the information you provide to resolve your request.

Legal bases and choices

Where GDPR applies, processing necessary to provide requested gameplay and ranking features is based on providing the service; security, fraud prevention and responding to support may rely on legitimate interests. Optional analytics and advertising processing requiring consent rely on consent. You can withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. In Settings, use Share gameplay analytics and Ad privacy options where available. Device privacy settings may also limit advertising identifiers. Refusing consent does not prevent basic gameplay.

Recipients and international transfers

Google provides Firebase and AdMob services and may process information outside your country under applicable safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where relevant. Advertising partners may receive information according to your consent choices. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request information. The website itself does not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

See Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, Google partner sites information and GitHub privacy statement.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until cleared or removed from the device. Online scores and identifiers may remain after uninstalling. We retain ranking records while needed to operate rankings and support information while needed to resolve requests or meet legal obligations. Google services apply their own configured retention periods. To request deletion of online data, email us with your nickname and relevant ranking details. We may need additional information to verify ownership. Do not send passwords or sensitive information.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing and withdraw consent. Contact us at the email above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority, including ANSPDCP in Romania.

Children

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review and address the request.

Changes

We will update this page when our practices change.
